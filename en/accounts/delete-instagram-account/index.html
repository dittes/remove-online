How to delete your Instagram account

Instagram lets you either deactivate an account (hidden, can come back) or delete it permanently. Deleting starts a countdown, so you can still change your mind.

Delete the account in the app

  1. Open Instagram, go to your profile and tap the menu (☰).
  2. Tap Accounts Center, then Personal details.
  3. Tap Account ownership and control, then Deactivation or deletion.
  4. Choose the account, select Delete account and tap Continue.
  5. Pick a reason, enter your password and confirm.

Download your data first

In Accounts Center, open Your information and permissions and choose Download your information. After deletion your photos and messages cannot be recovered.

What happens next

Instagram schedules the account for deletion and keeps it for a grace period (currently about 30 days). Logging in during that time cancels the deletion. Some copies in backups can take longer to disappear.

Tip: Not sure? Choose Deactivate instead. Your profile, photos and likes are hidden until you log back in.

Questions

Can I undo deleting my account?

Only during the grace period, by logging in again. After that the account and its content are gone.

Will my username become free?

Once the deletion is complete the username can be taken by someone else. Deactivating keeps it reserved.
