How to remove a Google account from your phone

Removing the account signs it out of Gmail, Play Store, Contacts and Calendar on this device. It does not delete the account itself.

On Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Passwords & accounts. On some phones it is called Accounts or Users & accounts.
  3. Tap the Google account you want to remove.
  4. Tap Remove account and confirm.
  5. If asked, enter your screen lock PIN, pattern or password.

On iPhone or iPad

If you added the account in iOS settings (Apple Mail, Contacts, Calendar):

  1. Open Settings and go to Mail, then Mail Accounts. On older iOS versions this is Passwords & Accounts.
  2. Tap the Google account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

If you only use the Gmail or Google app, tap your profile picture there and choose Manage accounts on this device, then remove the account. The wording differs a little between apps.

Warning: Selling or giving away an Android phone? Remove your Google account before you reset it. Otherwise Factory Reset Protection can ask the next owner for your login.

Questions

Does this delete my Google account?

No. The account and its data stay with Google. Only this phone loses access to it.

Why is there no “Remove account” button?

A work or school account may be managed by your organisation, and only the administrator can remove it. On some phones the account is also tied to a work profile.

Will I lose my photos and contacts?

Data stored in the account stays in the account. Items saved only on the phone, such as contacts stored locally, are not affected. Check your backup if you are unsure.
