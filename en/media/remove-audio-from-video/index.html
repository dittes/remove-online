How to remove the sound from a video

Mute a video for good, so that the exported file has no audio track, with your phone or a free tool.

iPhone

  1. Open the video in Photos and tap Edit.
  2. Tap the yellow speaker icon at the top left so it is crossed out.
  3. Tap Done.

Android (Google Photos)

  1. Open the video and tap Edit.
  2. Tap the speaker icon to mute the audio.
  3. Tap Save copy.

Mac (iMovie)

  1. Drag the clip onto the timeline.
  2. Choose Modify, then Detach Audio.
  3. Select the green audio bar and press Delete, then export.

Command line (FFmpeg)

This copies the picture without re-encoding it and drops the audio:

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -c copy -an output.mp4

Tip: Muting in the player only silences playback. To share a video with no sound at all, export a new file as described above.

Questions

Will the video quality change?

With the FFmpeg command above, no. Phone editors may re-encode the video slightly.

Can I replace the audio instead?

Yes. Remove the original track first, then add music or a voice-over in the same editor.
