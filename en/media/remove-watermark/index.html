How to remove a watermark (legally)

Removing a watermark from your own document or from a trial export is fine. Removing someone else’s from an image or video usually is not. Here is what works and what to do instead.

Watermarks in your own Word document

  1. Open the Design tab.
  2. Click Watermark.
  3. Choose Remove Watermark.

Watermarks in your own PDF

  1. Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat.
  2. Choose Edit PDF, then Watermark, then Remove.

Watermarks added by an app or trial

Video editors, design apps and conversion tools often add a logo on the free plan. The clean way to lose it is to upgrade, use the paid export, or re-export from the original project in an app that does not add a mark.

A photo or video that belongs to someone else

A watermark identifies the owner. Do not crop it out or paint it over. Instead:

  1. Ask the owner for a clean copy or permission.
  2. Buy a licence for the unwatermarked version from the stock site.
  3. Use images that are free to use, such as Wikimedia Commons or other public-domain and Creative Commons sources, and credit them as required.

Warning: In many countries, removing copyright information such as a watermark can be illegal, for example under § 95c of the German copyright act or § 1202 of the US DMCA, or Article 7 of EU Directive 2001/29/EC. This is general information, not legal advice.

Questions

Can I remove an AI watermark or label?

Labels that mark content as AI-generated exist so that others are not misled. We do not describe how to hide them.

What if I lost the original file without the watermark?

Check your cloud backup, your sent mail or the app’s export history. If you only have a watermarked copy of your own work, contact the tool’s support.
