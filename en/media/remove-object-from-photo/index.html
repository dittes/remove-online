How to remove an object or person from a photo

Phone galleries now include an eraser that fills in the gap automatically. Here is where to find it and how to get a clean result.

Where to find the eraser

Wording and availability depend on your phone, system version and region.

  1. Google Photos: open the photo, tap Edit, then Tools, then Magic Eraser.
  2. Samsung Galaxy: open the photo in Gallery, tap the pencil, then the Object eraser option.
  3. iPhone: on models with Apple Intelligence, tap Edit in Photos and choose Clean Up.
  4. Windows: on supported PCs, the Photos app offers Generative erase.
  5. Photoshop: use the Remove tool or Generative Fill.

How to get a clean result

  1. Zoom in and brush or circle the object, including its shadow.
  2. Remove small things first. Large objects in front of busy backgrounds are hard to fill convincingly.
  3. Apply, check the edges, and undo if the fill looks smeared.

Free option on a computer

GIMP is free and has a Heal tool and a Resynthesizer plugin for filling gaps. It needs more manual work than a phone eraser.

Warning: Edit your own pictures or ones you have permission to change. Removing people or objects to mislead others, for example in evidence or news photos, is not acceptable.

Questions

Does the original photo stay?

Most apps save the edit as a copy or let you revert. Check before you overwrite.

Why does the result look blurry?

The tool has to invent the missing pixels. Simple backgrounds like sky, grass or walls work best.
