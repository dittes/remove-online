How these guides work

  • Built-in tools first

    We start with what is already on your phone or computer, such as Preview, Photos, Word or your browser, before pointing you anywhere else.

  • Your files stay yours

    No guide asks you to upload a private document to a random website, and this site itself has no cookies or tracking.

  • Plain, honest steps

    Short numbered steps, the warning you need before you click, and a clear note when something cannot be undone.
