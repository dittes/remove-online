Erasure request generator (GDPR Article 17)

Write a ready-to-send request asking a website or company to delete your personal data, in English, German, Czech or Polish.

Loading the tool…

Runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

How it works

Fill in who you are, who should erase the data, and what and where it is. Choose the legal ground and the language of the letter. The text updates as you type and is built on your device only.

The letter cites Article 17 GDPR, asks the controller to inform other recipients (Articles 17(2) and 19) and sets the one-month deadline of Article 12(3). Send it by e-mail or post to the contact named in the site’s privacy policy or imprint, and keep a copy and proof of sending.

This is a template, not legal advice. For search results, also use the search engine’s own removal forms; see how to remove personal information from Google.

Questions

Who can I send it to?

Any organisation that processes your personal data and is subject to the GDPR, including website operators, shops and platforms that serve people in the EU/EEA. Companies elsewhere may follow their own rules.

What if they refuse or do not answer?

They must answer within one month (extendable in complex cases) and, if they refuse, say why. You can then complain to your data protection authority.

Is the text stored?

No. Nothing is sent or saved; closing the tab clears the form.
