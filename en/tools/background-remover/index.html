Background remover: make the background transparent

Cut out the subject of a photo, or remove a white or green-screen background, and download a transparent PNG. Nothing leaves your device.

Loading the tool…

Runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

How it works

AI cut-out uses a small segmentation model (U²-Net-small, Apache-2.0) that runs on your device. The first run downloads about 4.5 MB from this site, afterwards it works instantly. It is made for photos with a clear main subject such as people, products and animals; fine details like hair can be rough.

Colour mode removes one colour, such as a white studio background or a green screen. Click the picture or use the colour picker, then raise the tolerance until the background is gone. “Only connected to the edges” keeps matching colours inside the subject, for example a white shirt.

Large pictures are scaled down to 3000 pixels on the longest side. Save the result as PNG to keep the transparency, or fill the background with white or black.

Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The picture is processed by your browser; the model comes from this site and runs locally.

Why does the cut-out look rough?

The model is deliberately small so that it runs fast on any device. Try a photo with stronger contrast between subject and background, or use Colour mode for plain backgrounds.

Can I use the result commercially?

The tool puts no restriction on your pictures. Make sure you own or may edit the photo you use.
