Remove a white background from an image

Turn the white background of a logo, product photo or scan into transparency and download a PNG. Nothing is uploaded.

Loading the tool…

Runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

How it works

Drop an image with a white (or nearly white) background and press Remove background. The tool starts in Colour mode with white selected and removes it from the edges inward, so white areas inside your subject, such as a shirt, a highlight or the eye of a logo, stay as they are.

If a pale halo remains, raise the tolerance. If the cut eats into your subject, lower it. For photos of people or animals on busy backgrounds, switch to the AI cut-out.

Questions

Why does a white halo remain around my logo?

Anti-aliased edges contain pixels that are only partly white. Raise the tolerance a little until they disappear.

Can I keep white areas inside the image?

Yes. Keep “Only areas connected to the edges” ticked and only the outer white is removed.
