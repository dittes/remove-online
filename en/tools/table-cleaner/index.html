Table cleaner: remove duplicate rows and empty rows

Paste cells from Excel or Google Sheets, or open a CSV file, and remove duplicate rows, empty rows and empty columns.

Loading the tool…

Runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

How it works

Copy cells in Excel or Sheets and paste them on the left (the tab separator is detected automatically), or choose a CSV/TSV file. The cleaned table appears on the right, ready to copy back into your spreadsheet or to download.

Remove duplicate rows keeps the first occurrence. To compare only some columns, for example just the e-mail column, type their numbers: 1 or 1, 3 or 2-4. Leave it empty to compare entire rows. Tick First row is a header to protect the header row.

Values are handled as text, so leading zeros and long numbers stay exactly as they are.

Questions

Can I remove duplicates by one column only?

Yes. Enter the column number (1 is the first column) under “Compare only these columns”.

Does it open .xlsx files?

Not directly. Copy the cells from Excel and paste them, or save the sheet as CSV first.

Is my data uploaded?

No. It is processed in your browser only.
