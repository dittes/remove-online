Remove metadata (EXIF, GPS) from photos

See what is hidden in a JPG, PNG or WebP (location, camera, date) and download a clean copy. Lossless, no upload.

Loading the tool…

Runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

How it works

Drop one or several photos. The tool shows what it found, such as GPS location, camera model, date and software, and gives you a clean copy.

It cuts the metadata out of the file itself and copies the picture data unchanged, so there is no re-compression and no quality loss. Colour profiles are kept so colours look the same.

If a photo is stored rotated and relies on its orientation flag, only that single flag is kept so that it still displays upright.

Questions

Which formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP. Documents are covered by the PDF tool and by the steps in the guide.

Does it remove everything?

It removes EXIF (including GPS), XMP, IPTC and comments in JPG, text and EXIF chunks and the timestamp in PNG, and EXIF and XMP in WebP. The picture itself is not touched, so a visible watermark or a face stays in the image.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. They are read and rewritten in your browser.
