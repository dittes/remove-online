Remove characters from text: emoji, numbers, letters, first or last characters

Strip emoji, digits, punctuation, accents or any characters you choose, or cut the first or last characters off every line.

Loading the tool…

Runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

How it works

Paste text and tick what should go: emoji, digits, letters, punctuation, accents (é becomes e) or all non-ASCII characters. Type any characters in the custom field to delete exactly those.

Use Remove first N and Remove last N to cut characters from the start or end of every line, for example to drop a prefix or a 3-digit code from a whole column pasted from Excel.

Questions

How do I remove the first characters of every line?

Enter the number under “Remove first”. Each line loses that many characters from the left.

Does it remove emoji with skin tones and flags?

Yes. Skin-tone modifiers, flags and joined emoji are removed completely.
