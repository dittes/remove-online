How to remove personal information from Google search results

You can ask Google to stop showing results that contain your address, phone number or similar details. The page itself stays online unless you also contact its owner.

Skip the steps and do it right here: Erasure request letterOpen tool

Steps

  1. Search for your name, address and phone number to see what is visible today.
  2. Open Google’s Results about you tool: support.google.com/websearch/answer/12719076. In the Google app you find it under your profile picture.
  3. Add the contact details you want monitored, then review the results it finds.
  4. Next to a result, open the menu (⋮) and choose Remove result, then follow the questions.
  5. If a result is not covered, use Google’s “Remove information from Google” help form and describe the page.

Remove it at the source

Google only hides the result. To get the content off the internet, ask the website owner or host to delete the page. Once it is gone, the search result disappears too.

If you live in the EU or UK

You can also invoke your right to erasure (GDPR Article 17, “right to be forgotten”) through Google’s legal removal form for Europe. Google weighs your privacy against the public interest and may refuse.

Warning: Keep the confirmation e-mails and links to the pages you reported. You will need them if you have to appeal a decision.

Questions

How long does it take?

Google reviews each request, and the time varies. You get an e-mail when a decision is made.

What about Bing and other search engines?

Each search engine has its own removal form. Removing a result from Google does not remove it from them.
