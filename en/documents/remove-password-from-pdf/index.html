How to remove a password from a PDF

If you know the password and are allowed to open the file, you can save an unlocked copy so you no longer have to type it.

With Chrome or Edge

  1. Open the PDF in the browser and enter the password.
  2. Press Ctrl + P (⌘ + P on a Mac).
  3. Choose Save as PDF as the destination and click Save.
  4. The new file opens without a password.

With Preview on a Mac

  1. Open the PDF and enter the password.
  2. Choose File, then Export as PDF… and save a copy.

With Adobe Acrobat

  1. Open the PDF and enter the password.
  2. Choose File, then Properties, and open the Security tab.
  3. Set Security Method to No Security, click OK and save.

Warning: This only works when you know the password. A forgotten password cannot be recovered. Ask the person who created or sent the file. Do not remove restrictions from documents you have no right to use.

Questions

What is the difference between the two kinds of PDF password?

A document open password is needed to view the file. A permissions password only restricts printing or copying. The steps above start from a file you can already open.

Does the new copy look the same?

Printing to PDF can drop bookmarks and form fields. Preview and Acrobat keep them.
