How to remove pages from a PDF

You do not need to install anything or upload a document. Your Mac, your browser or Adobe Acrobat can delete pages from a PDF directly.

Skip the steps and do it right here: PDF page removerOpen tool

On a Mac with Preview

  1. Open the PDF in Preview.
  2. Choose View, then Thumbnails.
  3. Click the page you want to delete (hold ⌘ to select several).
  4. Press Delete, then save with ⌘ + S.

On Windows, Mac or Linux with Chrome or Edge

  1. Open the PDF in your browser and press Ctrl + P (⌘ + P on a Mac).
  2. As destination choose Save as PDF.
  3. Under Pages choose Custom and enter the pages you want to keep, for example 1-3, 5-8.
  4. Click Save. The result is a new file; your original stays untouched.

In Adobe Acrobat

  1. Choose Tools, then Organize Pages.
  2. Select the thumbnails you do not want and click the trash icon.
  3. Save the file.

Tip: Saving through the print dialog is the fastest way, but it can drop links, bookmarks and form fields. For those, use Preview or Acrobat.

Questions

Is it safe to use a website for this?

Free web tools upload your file to their server. For contracts, IDs or anything private, use one of the offline methods above.

Can I remove a page from a protected PDF?

Not until it is unlocked. See how to remove a password from a PDF.
